# Source-provenance addendum — commitment 2026-07-30

**The commitment itself is unchanged and remains valid.** `2026-07-30.commit.json` and its hash
`36c10459288a79dc7cec1d10243eb83d253f8d49e2a9983f974f790e99a6cdae` are exactly as published at

## What the commitment proves, and what it does not

It proves that a specific 39-pick official card, plus 90 separately-labelled research leans,
existed in that exact form before any game began. That property is intact and independently
verifiable: recompute the SHA-256 of the revealed card plus nonce and compare, then check the
GitHub Events API `PushEvent.created_at` for the commit that first published the file against
the `earliestEventStart` recorded in it.

It does **not** assert where each pick's market probability came from. The card predates
source tracking, which was added later the same day.

## What the numbers on that card actually were

On 2026-07-30 Pinnacle's prop coverage was degraded. The pull returned **16 props, strikeouts
only**, against a rolling 7-day median of **322**. Every other lane fell back to the Odds API
US+EU consensus (DraftKings/FanDuel medians).

Of the 332 priced rows on the board that day, **2% were genuinely Pinnacle** and the rest were
market consensus. So the model-versus-market edges attached to that card are, with the
exception of the strikeout rows, edges over soft books rather than over Pinnacle.

Two lanes on the card — Hits+Runs+RBI and Hits — are market-consensus by nature rather than by
outage. Pinnacle does not post those markets at all, so they were never Pinnacle comparisons on
any date.

## Why this addendum exists

Until 2026-07-30 the pipeline recorded every market probability in a field named `pinn_pct`
regardless of which book produced it, and the site published the pooled result as a
"vs Pinnacle" figure. Split by proven provenance the two populations behave very differently:
against Pinnacle the model realised **+0.6pp** against a claimed +7.9pp, while against market
consensus it realised **+4.6pp**. The blended headline therefore overstated the sharp-book
result, and it has been withdrawn from the site pending source-aware recomputation.

Rather than restate the card's edges, this addendum records the limitation. The honest summary
is that the 2026-07-30 card is a verified pregame commitment whose edges were measured
predominantly against market consensus, not against Pinnacle.

## Going forward

From 2026-07-30 every ledger row carries `market_pct` and a canonical `market_source` of
`pinnacle_direct`, `pinnacle_odds_api`, or `market_consensus`. Rows whose source cannot be
proven from the dated artifact that produced them are marked `unknown` and excluded from both
headline evaluations rather than assigned a source to preserve sample size.

Degraded Pinnacle coverage does not stop a board being published. It only forbids describing
those edges as Pinnacle.
